What's in the news, and how it's told

The Buffalo Times

The class news app collected a lot it never used. This project turns three of those missed opportunities into analysis: who the news is about, how much of it repeats itself, and whether the AI summary can be trusted. Every number on this page is computed from real headlines in saved NewsAPI snapshots.

EC 1 · Entity Explorer

Who is the news about?

spaCy entities were computed in class but never used. The small model mislabels Title Case headlines ("AI" as a country, "Donald Trump" as an organization), so a rule list corrects known names before counting. Bar color = average headline polarity.

EC 2 · Echo Index

Is today's news actually new?

Every headline is compared (TF-IDF cosine) with all earlier snapshots. Echo = similarity to its closest earlier headline.

    EC 3 · Newsroom Check

    Can we trust the AI summary?

    Gemini's JSON summary was never parsed in class. Here every headline is matched to the summary's story clusters (TF-IDF cosine ≥ ). In the app, weak claims are then checked with Tavily, Gemini rewrites the summary, the same metric re-scores it, and ElevenLabs reads it aloud.
